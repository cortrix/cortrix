# Maintainers

## Ownership and acknowledgment targets

| Surface | Responsible role | Primary | Backup | Intake path | Acknowledgment target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core bugs and documentation | Cortrix Core Maintainers | `admin@cortrix.ai` | Not assigned | GitHub Issues | Within 10 business days |
| Features and integrations | Cortrix Core Maintainers | `admin@cortrix.ai` | Not assigned | GitHub Issues | Within 10 business days |
| Security reports | Cortrix Security Response Team | `security@cortrix.ai` | Not assigned | Private email | Within 5 business days |
| Conduct and moderation | Cortrix Community Moderation Team | `devrel@cortrix.ai` | `community-maintainers` | Private email | Within 10 business days |

Acknowledgment means that the item was received and assigned an initial triage state. It is not a fix, merge, resolution, roadmap, or release commitment.

## Triage and recusal

- Maintainers label or state the initial disposition and request missing evidence when needed.
- Duplicate or off-topic public threads may be redirected or closed with a short reason. Abusive threads may be locked.
- A maintainer named in a conduct report must not access, investigate, decide, or close that report.
- `devrel@cortrix.ai` routes the report to an available, conflict-free member of the `community-maintainers` role. The role is a backup owner, not a separate public intake path.
- Independent escalation is not currently available. If no conflict-free maintainer is available, the report is acknowledged, preserved with limited access, and paused without a substantive decision until one is appointed.
