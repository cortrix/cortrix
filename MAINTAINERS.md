# Maintainers

## Ownership and acknowledgement targets

| Surface | Responsible role | Primary | Backup | Intake path | Acknowledgement target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core bugs and documentation | Cortrix Core Maintainers | `admin@cortrix.ai` | Not assigned | GitHub Issues | Within 10 business days |
| Features and integrations | Cortrix Core Maintainers | `admin@cortrix.ai` | Not assigned | GitHub Issues | Within 10 business days |
| Security reports | Cortrix Security Response Team | `security@cortrix.ai` | Not assigned | Private email | Within 5 business days |
| Conduct and moderation | Cortrix Community Moderation Team | `devrel@cortrix.ai` | Conflict-free maintainer appointed by `admin@cortrix.ai` | Private email | Within 10 business days |

Acknowledgement means that the item was received and assigned an initial triage state. It is not a fix, merge, resolution, roadmap, or release commitment.

## Triage and recusal

- Maintainers label or state the initial disposition and request missing evidence when needed.
- Duplicate or off-topic public threads may be redirected or closed with a short reason. Abusive threads may be locked.
- A maintainer named in a conduct report must not access, investigate, decide, or close that report.
- `admin@cortrix.ai` appoints a different, conflict-free Cortrix maintainer to own evidence access, escalation, and closure.
- If no conflict-free maintainer is available, the report is acknowledged and preserved with limited access, but no substantive decision is made until one is appointed.
